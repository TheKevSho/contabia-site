## Variant: Dense ledger

### Design stance
The v5 workbook, in the portal: one tree, Excel density, collapse on the left, freeze Concepto.

### Key choices
- Layout: sticky Concepto + scrollable period columns. Presente 15 cols, Modelado 27.
- Typography: Source Sans 3 / DM Serif (portal tokens).
- Color: REAL navy, QUINCENA sand, PROY. muted. Shadow band terracotta wash.
- Interaction: left `+`/`−` per section; Presente | Modelado tabs; palancas then cell override on Modelado.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Nick+Kevin+Claudia can sit on one grid. Matches the xlsx they already trust.
- Weak at: 15 columns still overflow a 13" laptop. 27 is a horizontal scroll, always.

### Best for
Thursday walk if the goal is “this is the model, not a dashboard poster.”
